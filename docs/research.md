# Research notes: what already exists

Searched November 2026. Listings were read but not installed or audited.

## Open source
| Project | What it does | Why it isn't enough here |
|---|---|---|
| [Grandma's Guardian](https://chromeboard.com/extension/grandmas-guardian-plmkmnjiplhldamhmfdbkemhlkcpikmp) | Blocks every site except an allowlist | Too restrictive for everyday browsing |
| [SaveGrandma](https://chromeboard.com/extension/savegrandma-email-securit-ijcnfjdhjnicghalfogndnkdiefomnpf) | Scans Gmail for phishing, locally | Email only; useful ideas for our Mail guard (Phase 2) |
| [SafeBrowse AI](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/phbpclfoogedgnlndkibojnbgdnpjdeh) | General scam/phishing/fake support numbers, no network calls | Not aimed at sales funnels; no caregiver role |

Check each project's license before reusing any code.

## Closed source (seniors)
TrustPause, SafePause, Senior Safe, SeniorShield.ai, GoldenShield, ScamBrake. Mostly phishing, fake
tech support, gift-card scams.

## Adjacent
- VeritasRx — checks supplement claims against literature, Amazon only.
- "Bullshit Detector" / TrustShield — highlights urgency phrases on any page.

## The gap ElderSafety fills
Nothing found that combines: detecting video-sales-letter and long-form supplement funnels; a
cooling-off pause at the moment of payment; evidence-based alternatives on the same topic; and a
caregiver who manages it remotely with a personal touch.

## Scale of the problem
The FTC's December 2025 report to Congress on older adults ([PDF](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P144400-OlderAdultsReportDec2025.pdf);
[news summary](https://www.investmentnews.com/practice-management/elder-fraud-losses-hit-3b-as-investment-scams-surge-42-ftc-says/268749)):
reported losses by people 60+ around $3B in 2025; investment scams ~$1.06B (+42%), romance ~$384M,
prizes ~$143M, tech support ~$126M — and older adults were 324% more likely than younger ones to
report losing money to tech-support scams.

Health products: the FTC has repeatedly acted against "cure" marketing aimed at seniors, e.g.
[Agora Financial's diabetes-cure publications (2019)](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2019/10/ftc-sues-publisher-targeting-seniors-phony-diabetes-cure-money-making-schemes)
and the [$26M Geniux memory-supplement settlement](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2019/04/18/FTC-settles-26-million-case-over-fraudulent-cognitive-support-claims).
[ClickBank's own blog](https://www.clickbank.com/blog/health-fitness-affiliate-marketing/) says health
& fitness is where most of its top sellers and affiliates operate (prostate, blood sugar, dental,
joint and weight-loss offers, commissions up to ~$200/sale) — which is why the checkout networks are
a strong signal.
